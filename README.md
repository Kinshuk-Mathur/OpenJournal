## 📖 About

Open Journal is a minimalist digital journaling web application designed to provide a peaceful space for your thoughts. With calming aesthetics, ambient background music, and an intuitive interface, it makes journaling a truly relaxing experience.

### ✨ Features

- 🎨 Calming aesthetic design with nature-inspired colors
- 🎵 Ambient background music (toggleable)
- 📝 Realistic paper and lines interface
- 🖼️ Add and position images in your journals
- 💾 Local storage - your journals stay private
- 📱 Fully responsive design
- 🔇 Toggle typing sounds and music
- 📚 Create and manage multiple journal entries

---

## 🎯 How to Use

1. **Create a Journal** - Click the '+' card on homepage
2. **Start Writing** - Type your thoughts with realistic typing sounds
3. **Add Images** - Click "📷 Add Memory" to attach photos
4. **Drag & Resize** - Move and resize images anywhere on the page
5. **Save** - Click "💾 Save" to store your entry
6. **View Journals** - All your entries appear as cards on the homepage

### Controls
- **🔊 Sound Button** - Toggle typing sounds (bottom right)
- **🎵 Music Button** - Toggle background music (above sound button)
